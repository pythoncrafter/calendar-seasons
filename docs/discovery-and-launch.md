**Description:** Personalize the Google Calendar website with spring, summer, autumn, and winter palettes, botanical illustrations, and optional gentle effects. Choose a season or let themes follow the seasons. Preferences stay on your device; Calendar Seasons does not read event data.

No public store listing or approval is established by this package.
